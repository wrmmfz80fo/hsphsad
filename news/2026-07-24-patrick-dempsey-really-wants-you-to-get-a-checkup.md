# Patrick Dempsey Really Wants You To Get a Checkup

I was just about to wrap up an interview with Patrick Dempsey when he stopped me to ask a question of his own: "How old are you?"

[Wants You To Get](https://pepnex.cc/category/bal9b8)

Don't worry, he wasn't mad. The famously handsome Emmy nominee simply wanted to make sure that I had been getting yearly checkups from my doctor, even though I'm still in my twenties. As it turns out, Patrick Dempsey, one of TV's most famous medical professionals, is very into discussing annual checkups.

[Wants You To](https://peplux.cc/dx9rxgd465/)

"It’s fascinating, isn’t it?" he told MensHealth.com. "You can track your body fat, your muscle growth. It becomes a game after a while, and that makes it interesting."

This is Dempsey's life now. After 12 years playing Dr. Derek Shepherd on Grey's Anatomy, the actor has largely turned his attention to actual medical awareness.

He continues to help run the Dempsey Center—the complimentary cancer care institute he founded in 2009 in honor of his mother, who died from ovarian cancer—and recently he partnered with global health service company Cigna for the "Page a TV Doc" initiative. The program allows you to page or text 1-833-PAGE-DOC to receive personal advice and reminders from famous TV doctors about preventative care.

[You To](https://pepzy.cc/category/dp8er77)

[You To Get Checkup](https://pipeprospect.com/category/erdp19n2i0)

"In your twenties, it’s so, so important to just kind of get a baseline from your doctor," Dempsey said. "Especially if you have a family history of any kind. Heart disease. Cancer. Anything like that. It takes a lot of anxiety away. If you’re healthy, you can take a deep breath and wait for the next year."

It's a mindset that just might save your life, especially considering that half of American men—who already have lower life expectancy than women—don't bother with annual checkups. Prostate cancer alone will affect 1 in 9 men at some point in their life, so it's crucial to make sure to see your doctor on a regular basis. Ignorance is not bliss when it comes to your health.

"I think the older I get, the healthier I get," says Dempsey. "But [it's because] I'm so much more conscious about my health."

[You To Get Checkup](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

The 52-year-old credits his continued health to, of all things, a love for racing extremely fast cars. (He was actually pretty damn good at it, too.) "I had to get a physical every year in order to do that, to get my racing license," Dempsey says. "In racing, your weight is critical. Your endurance is critical. And getting assistance, whether it’s with a nutritionist or your doctor, is just as important. That’s when it ... started to take off [for me] and I became much more aware."

Nowadays, it's less about a need for speed and more about staying consistent and staying healthy. Getting to the doctor is the first step, but you also need to stay active, which is the most basic form of preventative care. "Just exercise," Dempsey says. "Whatever that may mean for you. A sport you enjoy. Get to the gym. If you like pilates, yoga, walking, running, cycling. Just exercise."

Today is as good a day to start as any to start taking accountability for your health. The race car is optional.
