# Watch Busta Rhymes Do a Grueling Workout With Bodybuilder Kai Greene

Busta Rhymes is no stranger to the plates, and regularly posts updates about his weight training and fitness goals on social media. The rapper upped the intensity this week when he hit the gym with bodybuilder and personal trainer Kai Greene. Greene, an Arnold Classic winner and three-time contender in the renowned Mr. Olympia bodybuilding competition, shared a post-workout photo of their session on his Instagram.

A couple of days later, Busta shared some footage taken from their intense upper-body workout (or "painful beast out," as he called it).
