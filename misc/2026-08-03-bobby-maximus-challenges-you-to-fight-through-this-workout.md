Today's session is based on one of the things Maximus knows best: a UFC fight. The workout takes the same format as a standard UFC contest: three five minute rounds with one minute of rest in between each. You'll perform a few basic bodyweight movements, pile up reps, and hopefully avoid a knockout by the end of the fight. Depend on your stamina and smart pacing to last through the final bell.
