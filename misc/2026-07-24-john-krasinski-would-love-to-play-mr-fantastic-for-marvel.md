# John Krasinski Would 'Love' to Play Mr. Fantastic For Marvel

The internet put on its best Jim Halpert face when earlier this week John Krasinski hinted at a leap into the world of superhero movies.

The actor, who is deep into a press tour for his horror movie A Quiet Place, told Screenrant he would "love" to take on the role of Mr. Fantastic in a Fantastic Four reboot, provided his wife and Quiet Place co-star Emily Blunt got in on the spandex-clad action as Sue Richards, the Invisible Woman.

"I mean listen, I’m still getting into the whole superhero thing," Krasinski said. "I didn’t read comics as a kid, not as many, I read some but not as much, but I’m a huge superhero fan so yeah, I’d love to do something like that. If there are any left, a lot of these have been taken but the ones that are left, yeah, I would love to do something like that. And listen, I’d love to work with [Blunt] again so any chance I got would be great."

Years ago, Krasinski took a break from The Office to screen-test for the role of Captain America, a part that eventually went to a jacked up Chris Evans, pre-stache. Apparently, the sight of Chris Hemsworth's already-godly physique convinced the sitcom star that the Marvel life was not for him, as he told Conan O'Brien in 2016.
