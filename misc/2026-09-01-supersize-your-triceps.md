The practice of nailing horseshoes to hooves became commonplace about 2,000 years ago—around the same time the first modern mirror was invented. Ever since, men have been straightening their arms and craning their necks in the mirror to get a view of the coveted “horseshoe muscle.” (That would be your triceps.)

[mirror was](https://pipeprospect.com/h24tfgi2du/)

As the name suggests, your triceps consist of three parts, or “heads”—the long, lateral, and medial heads. “The separation you see on a well-developed tricep is because you’ve developed all three heads,” says Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., creator of the The 28-Day Fat Torch. “Working the medial head is the missing link. That’s what we want to target.”

The dumbbell triceps kickback works the entire triceps muscle, but it especially targets the medial head. Watch the video below to learn how to do the move with perfect form.

Bonus: The exercise also tightens your core. Because you’re holding one dumbbell in your hand, you have to engage your core to keep your body from twisting under the weight. “Any time you can incorporate a strength move into a core move there’s further benefit,” Durkin says. You’re also sitting back slightly for the move, which works your glutes and hamstrings.
