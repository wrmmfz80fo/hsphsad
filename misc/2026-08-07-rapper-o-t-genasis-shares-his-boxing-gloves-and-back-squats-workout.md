# Rapper O.T. Genasis Shares His Boxing Gloves and Back Squats Workout

Rapper O.T. Genasis is an entertainer, but don't let his flashy social media presence and slick videos fool you. He takes his fitness seriously, and not just so that he can have enough stamina for his live shows like so many other performers.

"Training helps me a lot when I'm onstage so I don't run out of breath, open my hips up when I'm onstage, running back and forth, because I am a performer," he told the Men's Health crew. "And most importantly, so I can whup somebody's ass."

Genasis, who you know from hits like "CoCo,""Cut It,"and "Everybody Mad," mixes hardcore fight training in disciplines like Muay Thai and boxing with heavy weight room work, so he's both scrap-ready and strong. The rapper invited the MH crew to hit the gym with him at Unbreakable Performance in L.A., to show off everything that goes into his training.

"We lift, we fight, we recover," he says. He's joined briefly by Jay Glazer, media personality and trainer, who helps to break down the fight training action.
