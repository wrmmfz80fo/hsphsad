# Testosterone, Body Fat, & Muscle Building

Testosterone isn't just for young men anymore, it helps us maintain valuable muscle mass as men age, as well as contributing to our overall well-being, fat burning, and positive attitudes.

But there is bad news about the typical American male's testosterone levels. Researchers from New England studied men from Boston and found that testosterone was decreasing with age at a faster rate than expected. What's worse is that the scientists could not explain this rapid decrease in testosterone.
