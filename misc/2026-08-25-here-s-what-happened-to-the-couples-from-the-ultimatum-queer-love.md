# Here’s What Happened to the Couples From 'The Ultimatum: Queer Love'

The Ultimatum is a relationship experiment like no other. Five couples come together, and within those couples, one of the partners has given their partner an ultimatum that they have to get married or they'll be breaking up for good. The couples then have a temporary break up where they pair off into trial marriages with new partners for three weeks, before getting back into their original couples for another three weeks.

After that, each person then decides if they want to get engaged to their original partner, enter into a relationship with someone new, or remain single. Queer Love is the first season to focus solely on queer couples, and in the ten episodes we’ve seen breakups, makeups, and even a love triangle.

Now that the season has wrapped, you may be wondering just what happened to the original couples, so we’re here with all the information. Here’s what happened to all the couples from The Ultimatum: Queer Love after the reunion.
