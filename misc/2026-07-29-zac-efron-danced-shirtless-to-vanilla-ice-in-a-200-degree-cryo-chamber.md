# Zac Efron Danced Shirtless to Vanilla Ice in a -200 Degree Cryo Chamber

It looks like Zac Efron is finding ways to enjoy himself during his recovery from ACL surgery. In a video he posted to his Instagram story earlier this week, the actor stripped down to his swimming trunks and danced around to ‘Ice Ice Baby’ by 90s rapper Vanilla Ice with friends in a below freezing cold cryotherapy chamber.
