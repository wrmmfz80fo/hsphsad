But density—intensity’s evil cousin—will help you maximize your results.

Density describes how much work you’ll accomplish in a given period of time. The more work you do in the same number of minutes, the more fat you’ll demolish and the greater your metabolic boost.

Take the workout shown in the video above. I call it the Dumbbell Density Doomsday. It only consists of 4 movements, but it packs as much work as possible into it and works your entire body for a non-stop calorie burn.

Directions: Perform the following movements as a circuit, moving from one exercise to the next without resting. Do as many rounds as possible in 10 to 30 minutes.

1. Walking dumbbell lunges, 25 yards 2. Dumbbell pushups, 10 reps 3. Backward walking dumbbell lunges, 25 yards 4. Dumbbell pushup rows, 10 reps
