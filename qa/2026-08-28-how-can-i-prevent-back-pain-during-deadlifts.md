No exercise trains your back quite like a deadlift does. Before you give up on it, figure out if your pain is caused by either of these common problems. They're both easy to fix.

1. Most men have a strength imbalance between their lower and upper back, causing one to give out before the other does. If you're performing a high number of repetitions, your weak spot will fatigue first, so either your upper back will start to round forward or your lower back will begin to give. Either case will cause you pain.

[pain during deadlifts](https://raazpeptide.com/kpikyqdo5t/)

The fix: Perform fewer repetitions. Try 3 to 5 sets of 4 to 6 reps.

2. If you lack the hip mobility needed to assume the proper position, you'll bend too far forward at the waist instead of keeping your torso upright.

The fix: Swap your barbell for dumbbells. This changes your center of gravity so you won't bend forward quite as far.
