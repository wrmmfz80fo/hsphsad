# Bear Grylls Puts His Life in Your Hands in Netflix's New Interactive Show

Following the success of 2018’s Black Mirror: Bandersnatch, Netflix announced that it would be investing in developing more “choose your own adventure” content—and we now know that its next interactive series will be an immersive expedition experience hosted by famous adventurer Bear Grylls.

You Vs. Wild was announced during an executive sit-down with Netflix’s original programming team at the streaming company’s headquarters in Los Angeles, Deadline reports. The announcement was accompanied by an interactive trailer for the new eight episode series, in which viewers have the option to answer or decline the “call of the wild”… aka, a FaceTime call from Bear Grylls.
